# Sources and limits

This package synthesizes general methods in original wording. Source links are references, not endorsements, required subscriptions or included third-party content. Accessed 14 October 2026.

## Practitioner methods

- [Karri Saarinen: Brand is who you are](https://karrisaarinen.com/posts/brand-is-who-you-are/): beliefs become meaningful through decisions and actual work. Practitioner judgment, not causal proof.
- [Design Council: The Double Diamond, 15 years on](https://designcouncil.org.uk/our-work/projects-programmes/archive/double-diamond-15-years): iterative discovery/definition and development/delivery, not an irreversible sequence.
- [Stanford: Design Thinking Bootleg](https://vpge.stanford.edu/resources/design-thinking-bootleg): generation, prototypes and testing. Its cards are not included here.
- [IDEO.org: Analogous Inspiration](https://designkit.org/methods/analogous-inspiration.html): look outside the category for transferable relationships.
- [Marty Neumeier: The Onlyness Test](https://www.martyneumeier.com/the-onlyness-test): optional strategy provocation, not permission to claim unsupported exclusivity.
- [NN/g: Four Dimensions of Tone of Voice](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/tone-of-voice-dimensions/): observable tone differences; not a universal personality taxonomy.
- [NN/g: Microsoft Desirability Toolkit](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/microsoft-desirability-toolkit/): balanced reaction vocabulary and interpretation. We do not reproduce the original toolkit or claim its original paper/rights were independently verified.
- [Ehrenberg-Bass: Brands of Distinction](https://marketingscience.info/news-and-insights/brands-of-distinction): learned associations, uniqueness and consistency. New assets need exposure; visual novelty is not established recognition.

## Empirical findings, with boundaries

- [Sio, Kotovsky & Cagan (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.destud.2015.04.004): design examples can support novelty while narrowing categories explored. References can inspire and fixate simultaneously.
- [Lucero (2012)](https://doi.org/10.1145/2317956.2318021): qualitative study of moodboards as framing, alignment, paradox, abstraction and direction. Not causal proof of brand outcomes.
- [Korde & Paulus (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jesp.2016.09.009): alternating individual/group ideation can help idea generation in experimental tasks. Not proof of commercial identity success.
- [Wadinambiarachchi, Kelly, Pareek, Zhou & Velloso (2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.11164): AI-image inspiration increased fixation in a novice visual-ideation task. Do not generalize to every model, designer or brand workflow.
- [Doshi & Hauser (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adn5290): individual creative gains and reduced collective diversity in short fiction. Not a branding experiment.

Independent associations, source diversity and downstream AI use are proposed safeguards, not proven cures for homogenization. No single score or exact worksheet sequence here has been validated as a predictor of brand success.

## Technical standards

- [W3C: Contrast (Minimum)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html)
- [W3C: Non-text Contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html)

Qualifying large text is at least 18 pt, or 14 pt bold, under the text criterion. Check actual usage, states, opacity and backgrounds; do not round a failing ratio. These criteria are not a full accessibility audit.
