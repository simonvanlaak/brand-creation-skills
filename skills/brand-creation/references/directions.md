# Direction and refinement

## 4. Explore independent directions before implementation

Start rough. Generate independently before group ranking; share associations, then allow another individual pass. Use different source material or strategic hypotheses to avoid producing one idea in several colours. AI imagery is optional and downstream—not a required opening move.

Show comparable identity specimens with the same real content. Directions should differ in organizing idea, composition, type behaviour, image-making and voice. Include a credible unconventional option, not a deliberately bad straw man. Each direction states why it belongs, what it refuses and its likely failure.

Preserve a private reviewable trail. Select one direction with reasons; do not blend every favourite into a committee compromise. Exploratory applications can expose problems early, but production website construction waits for identity agreement.

**Output/gate:** selected direction and rejected alternatives with reasons. Return to discovery if no direction expresses the brief.

## 5. Refine without widening approvals

Compare type, palette, wordmark, imagery and voice while holding unrelated variables steady. Batch independent questions for speed. Approval of a typeface does not approve its specimen's layout. Record exact accepted values, pending choices and superseded decisions.

Check palette feasibility before locking it. If outputs repeatedly look artificial, change production method or source material rather than requesting more 'authentic' styling. Keep real provenance; generated handwriting is not the owner's handwriting.

**Output/gate:** accepted rules and master assets, useful copy examples, rights record and scoped decision log.
