# Brand creation

## Overview

Standardize the process, not the personality. Help a human find what is true, express it distinctly, and deliver an identity that works. Do not replace their taste with a model's default aesthetic.

Separate conviction (what we act on), differentiation (a credible reason to choose us), and recognition (cues people learn). Values need not be individually unique to matter.

## When to use

Use for a new personal, company or community identity; an evidence-led rebrand; or a weak identity that needs a clearer foundation. For a narrow change to an approved system, read its current rules and use only the relevant stage. Do not reopen approved foundations without a reason.

## Start here

1. Read the authorized existing brand rules, work, references and decisions. Ask before accessing private sources outside the agreed scope.
2. Establish purpose, decision owner, audience where relevant, desired deliverables and publication boundary. Do not turn self-expression into a sales funnel.
3. Create a private project workspace with sources, decisions, specimens and checks. Never put client research in this skill repository.
4. Record current stage, accepted decisions, unresolved hypotheses, source references and next consequential question. Use [project state](templates/project-state.md).
5. Load only the next relevant stage below. Ask a few consequential questions per round; avoid a long questionnaire where existing evidence already answers it.

## Stage routing

| Stage | Read | Completion gate |
|---|---|---|
| Discover, compare, define | [Discovery](references/discovery.md) | Owner confirms purpose, foundations, relationships and uncertainties |
| Explore and refine | [Directions](references/directions.md) | Genuinely different premises considered; exact accepted choices recorded |
| Evaluate and verify | [Validation](references/validation.md) | Separate owner, audience and technical results with limits |
| Publish the system | [Delivery](references/delivery.md) | Authorized public artifacts agree and work at their deployed URLs |

Stages may loop. Cheap exploratory applications can test identity early; production integration waits for approval. If a gate lacks evidence, report the missing evidence instead of inventing completion. When publication or deployment is not requested, stop at the approved local handoff and label it accurately.

## Working rules

- Capture lived evidence and unprompted associations before model-created concepts. Explain what a reference contributes, rather than copying its recognizable features.
- The agent researches actual peer surfaces and asks how this identity should relate and differ. Do not invent customer insights or claim unsupported uniqueness.
- Generate independent conceptual premises. Palette swaps do not constitute divergence. Image generation and random seeds are optional tools, not requirements.
- Keep direct statements, observations, interpretations and aspirations separate. Mark agent critiques as agent critiques, never participant findings.
- Scope approvals narrowly and preserve rejected choices with reasons. Never silently change approved colours, fonts or assets to make a test pass.
- Use real or correctly attributed assets. Generated handwriting is not someone's actual handwriting. Access to a reference is not permission to reproduce it.
- Maintain different evidence for preference, comprehension, recognition and accessibility. A numerical model score does not select the right identity.
- Do not request passwords or tokens in project notes. Respect privacy, source licences and publication authorization.

## Common pitfalls

- Starting with a finished website: prototype small identity specimens first.
- Inferring visual taste from a favourite book or album: ask what the owner likes about it.
- Smoothing away contradictions: keep meaningful tensions and test expression.
- Demanding immediate recall for a new identity: examine peer confusion now and recognition after exposure.
- Repairing artificial output with more adjectives: change source material or production method.
- Treating a successful build as publication: verify actual routes, downloads and content.
- Copying third-party worksheets: use the original blank worksheets here, and verify rights for additional materials.

## Verification checklist

- [ ] Each major choice has evidence or is labelled a hypothesis.
- [ ] Directions differ in premise, not just decoration.
- [ ] Exact approvals and unresolved decisions are recorded.
- [ ] Actual applications and permitted colour pairs have been checked.
- [ ] Owner acceptance is separate from audience findings and technical results.
- [ ] Rights and privacy reviewed for every public asset.
- [ ] Delivery status reflects what was actually exercised and observed.

## Evidence limits

This is a research-informed workflow, not a validated guarantee of originality or commercial success. AI creativity studies concern specific writing and visual-ideation tasks, not this full branding process. Read [sources and limits](references/sources.md) before making research claims. No particular user's aesthetic or private history is required.
